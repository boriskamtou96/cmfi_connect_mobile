## How the text would be used

- Read inside the app's Bible reader, **one chapter at a time, online only**:
  the text is fetched from the licensed delivery channel when a chapter is
  opened and is **never stored on the device** (no offline download, no
  full-text search or concordance on this version).
- Verbatim text only: no editing, paraphrase or adaptation.
- The copyright notice is shown under every chapter (we will use the exact
  wording you provide), along with any usage reporting the channel requires
  (e.g. API.Bible FUMS).
- **No artificial intelligence or machine learning** use of any kind.
- No advertising. The Bible reader, including this version, is **free for
  every user**.

## Commercial status

The app is free to download. It offers an **optional Premium subscription**
(a home-screen widget, a yearly report summary, goal progress charts). The
Bible text is **not** part of Premium and is never sold or used as an
incentive. Because the app has a subscription, we understand it does not
qualify for Express Licensing, hence this request.

As a ministry tool serving a missionary community, we kindly ask whether the
royalty can be waived or reduced.
